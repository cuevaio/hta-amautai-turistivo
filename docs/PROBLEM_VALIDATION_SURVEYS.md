## Current version

Both surveys cover grades 1-6 of primary education in Peru. Second grade is a presentation demo case, not the product's scope limit.

Each survey contains seven questions: grade, frequency of needing help in the past four weeks, the most recent main difficulty, one concrete example, the first action taken, the observed outcome, and optional contact permission and details. Four questions use options; two ask for a one-sentence account; the last is optional contact information.

The design follows The Mom Test: recent behavior and concrete experiences rather than hypothetical product approval. The frequency question uses a five-point ordinal scale plus an unobserved option. Estimated completion time is 2-3 minutes; it has not been measured.

## Pitch evidence

Report teachers and families separately, state counts as X of N respondents, and use real anonymized examples. Survey answers support problem discovery, not claims of learning improvement or product efficacy. Exclude contact details from presentations. No response results are recorded in this file; inspect current submissions before making claims.
